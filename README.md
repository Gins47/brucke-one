# Brücke One

Brücke One is an AI-assisted emergency shelter coordination proof of concept.

Its goal is to help a local relief organization move affected households to a safe, suitable shelter quickly—without overwhelming available capacity.

## What it will support

- Emergency operations, shelters, and operation-specific shelter activations.
- Accurate operational capacity, occupancy, reservations, and availability.
- Help requests with usable location information and essential accommodation needs.
- Deterministic, capacity-safe shelter matching and reservations.
- Clear arrival instructions with a reservation reference or QR code.
- Volunteer check-in that keeps shelter occupancy up to date.
- AI-assisted intake, translation, and explanations, with operator oversight for large, uncertain, or high-risk cases.

## Core principle

AI can help collect information and explain outcomes, but it never controls shelter capacity or makes unrestricted safety decisions. The application enforces eligibility, capacity, and reservations through deterministic, transactional domain logic.

## Current scope

This is a focused local-organization PoC, not a replacement for government emergency systems or emergency services. Medical triage, autonomous dispatch, inventory logistics, public alerts, and broad multi-agent automation are outside the initial scope.
